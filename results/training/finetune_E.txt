---------------------------------------------------------------------
COMMON SETTINGS OF RUN 2
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Data                 Learn2Reg Lung CT, intra-patient pairs expiration (moving) -> inspiration (fixed)
Training             17 patients (case_004-case_020) + inverted pairs = 34 pairs
Validation           3 patients (case_001, case_002, case_003), landmark TRE in mm on the native grid
TRE without reg.     14.65 mm (validation mean)
Point convention     training CSVs with x,y axes flipped (LPS vs RAS) and phases _0000/_0001 swapped
                     with respect to the images; verified with the correlation of the patches around the points
                     (0.80 keypoints, 0.76 landmarks versus 0.07-0.28 for the previous convention).
                     The runs before the correction (run 1) used wrongly matched points and their TREs
                     are not comparable.
Resolution           TRAIN_TARGET_SHAPE = (192, 192, 192)
Batch                BATCH_SIZE = 1, NUM_WORKERS = 10, data copied to a local disk
Model                SwinUNETR (feature_size 48, 62.2 M parameters), MONAI self-supervised weights,
                     Conv3d head 16->3 initialized to ~0 (initial DVF = identity),
                     stationary velocity field integrated with scaling and squaring (9 steps)
Precision            bfloat16 (NCC in float32), gradient clipping at 1.0
Loss                 SuperRegistrationLoss: NCC (window 7, masked on the lungs) + 0.5 x MIND,
                     multiscale 100% and 50%; label Dice 0.4 (DIFFERENTIABLE_DICE = True);
                     DVF smoothness, inverse consistency (lambda_inv = 0.5), landmark loss
                     (mean error in mm) and keypoint loss (smooth L1 in mm) with linear curriculum weights
Saving               model with the best validation TRE in best_reg_model.pth,
                     resume checkpoint at every epoch

---------------------------------------------------------------------
MOTIVATION
---------------------------------------------------------------------
After FINETUNE_D the network fits the training set (41%) but does not generalize (20% in validation).
With the shared rigid motion, wider rotations and translations add no fake motion:
they only increase the variety of poses seen by the network. Only change with respect to FINETUNE_D.

                     weight_decay = 1e-4
Scheduler            linear warmup 3 epochs + cosine, total_epochs = 300, min_lr_ratio = 0.01

Curriculum           smooth (1.05, 1.5) | landmark (0.6, 1.5) | keypoint (2.1, 3.0)

Initial threshold    best_val = 11.86 mm
Augmentation         aug_same_affine = True, aug_max_rot = 0.10 rad, aug_max_trans_vox = 8.0;
                     rigid motion p 0.8, flip 0.5, intensity 0.3

Conclusion on training: no generalization gain with respect to FINETUNE_D. With 17 training patients
the network stops around 11.85 mm; going further would need a structural change
(for example two-stage registration for the large displacements of case 001).

Notes
- The TTO reduces the TRE by 80% with respect to no registration, also on case 001.
- The folding (percentage of voxels with det(J) <= 0, computed on the whole volume) is too high
  for clinical use: with lambda_reg = 0.01 the regularization in the TTO is effectively absent.
- Next step: TTO with lambda_reg in (0.01, 0.5, 2.0), measuring TRE and folding on the whole
  volume and inside the lungs (section 7 of the notebook).
