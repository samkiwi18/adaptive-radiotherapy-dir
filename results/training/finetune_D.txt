Interpretation: the network learns little even on the training pairs (a learning limit, not a
generalization limit) and applies an "average" displacement: almost none on the patients with little motion.

Cause found: the augmentation applied independent rigid motions to moving and fixed, adding
between the two images a random misalignment of the same order as the breathing motion
(up to about 7.5 voxels of change in the relative displacement of the points in a controlled test).
Fix: new dataset parameter aug_same_affine = True, which applies the same rigid motion
to moving and fixed (change in the relative displacement = 0 in the same test).
