8. NOTES AND LIMITATIONS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 - Plan computed with the pencil beam engine (SVDPB); in lung, pencil beam
   algorithms are less accurate than convolution/superposition or Monte Carlo,
   especially in the penumbra and at lung-tumour interfaces.
 - Dose grid 3 mm; ICRU 91 / AAPM recommend <= 2 mm for SBRT. If the final
   dose is recomputed at 2 mm, the values in section 6 change slightly.
 - Numbers refer to the run in GNU Octave; MATLAB may give slightly different
   weights (floating point, optimizer stopping point).
 - Before rerunning compute_dose.m delete plan_reference.mat and *_dose.mat
   (or set forceReoptimization / forceRecalculation to true).
